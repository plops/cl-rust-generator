- **Snell's Law in Vector Form:** Given an incident ray direction \(\vec{d}\), a surface normal \(\vec{n}\), and refractive indices \(n_1\) and \(n_2\):
  \[\mu = \frac{n_1}{n_2}\]
  \[\cos(\theta_1) = -\vec{n} \cdot \vec{d}\]
  \[\vec{d}_{\text{refracted}} = \mu \vec{d} + \left(\mu \cos(\theta_1) - \sqrt{1 - \mu^2 (1 - \cos^2(\theta_1))}\right) \vec{n}\]
