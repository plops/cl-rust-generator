can i run this on my phone without paying $100 to apple?

**Yes, absolutely.** You do not need to pay Apple $99/year to run your own app on your own iPhone. 

Apple allows anyone with a standard, **free Apple ID** to sign and install apps (this is called "personal provisioning" or "sideloading"). 

---

### The Two Caveats of a Free Apple ID
Before looking at the methods, keep Apple’s free tier limits in mind:
1. **7-Day Expiration:** Every app installed via a free Apple ID is valid for **7 days**. After 7 days, it won't launch unless it is "refreshed" (re-signed).
2. **3-App Limit:** You can only have up to **3 sideloaded apps** active on your device at any given time.

Tools like **SideStore** or **AltStore** can refresh the app automatically over Wi-Fi, so the 7-day limit is manageable.

---

### Prerequisites on Your iPhone (iOS 16+)
If your iPhone is running iOS 16, 17, or 18, you must enable **Developer Mode**:
1. Go to **Settings** > **Privacy & Security**.
2. Scroll to the very bottom and tap **Developer Mode**.
3. Toggle it on and restart your iPhone when prompted.
4. After restarting, tap **Turn On** and enter your passcode.

---

### Method 1: Sideloadly (Easiest & Fastest — 5 minutes)
*Best if you just want to plug your phone into a PC (Windows or Mac), install the `.ipa`, and test it immediately.*

1. Download and install **[Sideloadly](https://sideloadly.io/)** on your computer (Windows or macOS).
2. Connect your iPhone to your computer using a USB cable (tap "Trust This Computer" on your phone if asked).
3. Download the unsigned `.ipa` generated by your GitHub Actions release.
4. Open Sideloadly:
   * Drag and drop the `.ipa` file into the box on the left.
   * Enter your **Apple ID** email.
   * Click **Start**.
5. Sideloadly will prompt for your Apple ID password (and 2FA code) so it can generate a free 7-day development certificate directly from Apple.
6. Once it says *Done*, the app appears on your home screen.
7. **Trust the Certificate (First time only):**
   * On your iPhone: Go to **Settings** > **General** > **VPN & Device Management**.
   * Under *Developer App*, tap your Apple ID email.
   * Tap **Trust "[Your Apple ID]"**.

---

### Method 2: SideStore (Best Wire-Free / PC-Free Experience)
*Best if you don't want to connect your phone to a computer every week. SideStore runs entirely on your phone and re-signs apps over local Wi-Fi.*

1. **One-time Setup:**
   * Install **SideServer** on your PC/Mac to push **SideStore** to your iPhone the first time.
   * Follow the official [SideStore Guide](https://sidestore.io/).
2. **Using the App:**
   * Once SideStore is installed on your phone, you don't need the computer anymore.
   * Open Safari on your iPhone, go to your GitHub repository’s **Releases**, and download `lbw-client-unsigned.ipa`.
   * Open the **SideStore** app, go to **My Apps**, tap the `+` icon, and select the `.ipa` file you just downloaded.
   * SideStore signs and installs it right on your phone.
   * As long as you connect to your home Wi-Fi and activate the local WireGuard VPN loopback configured by SideStore, it will automatically renew your 7-day certificate in the background.

---

### Method 3: AltStore (The Classic Option)
Similar to SideStore, but requires **AltServer** running on a computer connected to the same Wi-Fi network to refresh your apps automatically every few days.

1. Install **AltServer** on your computer from [altstore.io](https://altstore.io/).
2. Connect your phone via USB and choose **Install AltStore** onto your phone.
3. Download the `.ipa` onto your phone from GitHub Releases.
4. Share/open the `.ipa` into the **AltStore** app to install it.
5. Whenever your phone and computer are on the same Wi-Fi network, AltStore will silently refresh the 7-day certificate in the background.

---

### Summary: What Workflow to Pick?

* **For quick testing:** Use **Sideloadly** via USB. Download your `.ipa` artifact from GitHub Actions and drop it in.
* **For a daily-driver app:** Set up **SideStore** once. After that, you can download `.ipa` updates straight from GitHub Releases onto your phone and install/renew them without a cable.
